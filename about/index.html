About Capture RVA

We're a Richmond photo booth company that started with one idea: the best part of a party should be something you can keep.

Modern booths, made for Richmond's events

Capture RVA brings the Salsa 2 photo booth and themed accessories to weddings, birthdays, community events and company parties across the Richmond metro. We've set up in Richmond, Midlothian, Powhatan, Williamsburg and Charlottesville, for clients from local showrooms to dance studios to chambers of commerce.

Every event gets a start screen designed for it, a backdrop that suits the room and a private online gallery afterward. We show up early, set up clean and make it easy for guests to jump in.

Our mission

Give every guest an easy, fun way to be part of the memories, and give every host photos worth keeping.

What we care about

Creativity, quality and customer care, from the first email to the last photo in your gallery.

Where we're headed

To be the first name people in Virginia think of when they want a photo booth at their event.
